About us

Ecole Sainte Bernadette Kamonyi

History. Founded by the Catholic parish of Kamonyi to serve local families, our school has grown from a small primary school into a full Nursery–Primary–Secondary institution known across the district for discipline and results.

Profile. Catholic day school · Kamonyi District, Southern Province, Rwanda · trilingual environment (Kinyarwanda · English · French).

🎯 Mission

Quality holistic education rooted in faith.

🔭 Vision

A model school of faith, discipline and academic excellence.

💎 Core values

Faith · Discipline · Excellence · Integrity · Service.

👥 Leadership

Head Teacher · Deputy in charge of Studies · Deputy in charge of Discipline · Bursar · School Chaplain · Heads of Departments.

📖 Academic philosophy

Every child can succeed: close individual follow-up, remedial support, and continuous assessment — CATs, mid-term, end-term and mock examinations.

🏫 Facilities

Modern classrooms · science laboratory · library · ICT room · chapel · playground · canteen · clean water & sanitation.

Academics

Learning journey

🧸 Nursery

Socialisation, language awakening, motor skills and early faith.

✏️ Primary (P1–P6)

Kinyarwanda, English, French, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, Religion.

📗 Lower Secondary — O-Level (S1–S3)

Mathematics, English, Kinyarwanda, French, Kiswahili, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, History, Geography, ICT, Entrepreneurship, Religion.

📕 Upper Secondary — A-Level (S4–S6)

Science combinations (PCM, PCB), technology & business (MCE — Mathematics, Computer Science, Economics) and humanities & languages (MEG, HEG, HGL): Advanced Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computer Science, English Literature, French, Kinyarwanda, History, Geography, Economics, General Paper, ICT, Entrepreneurship.

Assessment

Examinations

CATs · Mid-term · End-term · Mock · Final examinations.

A ≥ 80 B ≥ 70 C ≥ 60 D ≥ 50 F < 50 — pass mark 50%.

🗓 Academic calendar 2025–26

Term 1: Sep–Dec · Term 2: Jan–Mar · Term 3: Apr–Jul.
Mid-term examinations start 20 October.
